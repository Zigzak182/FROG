🐸 Who's that frog

Meet the Doctor of the Swamp

Deep in the fog where the cattails whisper, there's a lily pad clinic with a single flickering lantern. The sign outside reads “DR. FROGMAN — Walk-ins Welcome — No Herons.”

Armed with a head mirror he found in a drainage pipe and a stethoscope he absolutely did not steal, Dr. Frogman diagnoses everything as “a bit damp” and prescribes the same cure every time: vibes, flies, and one more game.

When he's not hopping between pads, he streams — co-op chaos, cozy swamp nights, and the occasional deeply questionable karaoke ribbit.

📜 Swamp lore

The Legend of the Frogman

The Tadpole Years

Born in a puddle no bigger than a teacup, a tiny tadpole spent his days watching the old heron-doctor of the marsh patch up turtles. He took notes. In mud. They washed away. He kept going anyway.

The Great Drought

When the swamp began to dry, it was a young frog with a shiny mirror on his head who hopped pad to pad, keeping everyone hydrated and entertained. The swamp folk started calling him Doctor. He did not correct them.

The Glowing Rectangle

One night a firefly-lit screen washed up in the reeds. The Doctor discovered he could broadcast his clinic to the whole world. The pond grew. The ribbits grew louder. The rest is history (still being written, live, most nights).
